BlueFairy Recipes — Marketing Content
=====================================


3-SENTENCE SUMMARY
------------------

BlueFairy Recipes turns WordPress into a recipe site: recipe pages with ingredients, steps, timings, servings, nutrition and a photo or video, a recipe archive filtered by meal type, diet, season and cuisine, writer profiles with public pages, and Recipe structured data for Google. Four blocks, three shortcodes and a read-only REST endpoint come with it. Requires PHP 8.1+ and WordPress 6.7+; licensed GPL-2.0-or-later; free.


CSS CLASS PREFIX
----------------

bfrc   (Recipes; the Pro add-on uses bfrcp)
